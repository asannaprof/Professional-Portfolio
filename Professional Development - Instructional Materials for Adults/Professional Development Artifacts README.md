# Professional Development Supplements

The items in this folder were created as supplements to professional development sessions I developed and led for colleagues. They demonstrate my ability to research complex topics, organize information effectively, and deliver professional training materials using industry-standard tools.

## Contents Overview

This folder includes instructional materials created to support adult learning in professional environments. All materials are designed following adult learning principles (andragogy) and formatted using Microsoft Office tools including Word, PowerPoint, and Visio.

## Key Skills Demonstrated

- **Microsoft Office Proficiency** - Expert use of Word, PowerPoint, and Visio for creating professional documentation and presentations
- **Adult Learning Principles** - Materials designed with andragogy principles, recognizing the unique learning needs of adult learners
- **Professional Facilitation** - Clear, well-organized materials that support live training delivery
- **Training Material Development** - Comprehensive instructional design including guides, visual aids, and reference materials
- **Complex Topic Translation** - Ability to research and distill complex subjects into accessible, actionable content

## How to Use These Materials

1. **Review individual materials** - Each document stands alone as a supplement to a specific training session
2. **Follow the organization** - Materials are organized by topic/session for easy navigation
3. **Adapt as needed** - These templates and materials can serve as models for your own professional development content

## Document Types

This folder may contain:
- **Facilitation Guides** - Outlines and speaker notes for training delivery
- **Participant Handouts** - Accompanying materials for trainees to reference during and after sessions
- **Visual Aids** - PowerPoint presentations, diagrams, and flowcharts created in Visio
- **Reference Documents** - Supplementary information, templates, and resources
